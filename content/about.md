I'm **Sorin Platon**, a VCF Solution Architect. I came up through cloud networking
on VMware Cloud Foundation, NSX and Kubernetes, and these days the work covers the
whole VCF stack. Before Broadcom's acquisition I was a Senior Solution Specialist for
Virtual Cloud Network at VMware.

Credentials: CCIE Service Provider, CCIE Routing & Switching, Nokia NRS II, VCIX-NV.

The posts here are field notes on VCF, end to end:

- Networking: NSX T0/T1 design, BGP traffic engineering, EVPN/VXLAN, NSX
  Federation, DFW, multi-tenancy with NSX Projects, TEP-less VPCs.
- Kubernetes: VKS, the Supervisor, self-service through VCF Automation,
  GitOps with Argo CD.
- Operations: VCF Operations, the offline depot, lifecycle, and the AI
  Assistant running on a private LLM.
- Holodeck: the nested VCF 9.1 lab all of the above is built on. It puts the whole
  stack on one host, and I tear it down and rebuild it as often as an idea needs.

I write everything up as working configs with the traps called out, because that's
what I wished I'd found when I was searching. If a post says a config works, it ran
on the Holodeck first.

Based in Quebec. I write in English; I'll answer in French too.
