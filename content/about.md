**Sorin Platon** is a VCF Solution Architect who came up through cloud networking
on VMware Cloud Foundation, NSX and Kubernetes; these days the work covers the
whole VCF stack. Before Broadcom's acquisition, Sorin was a Senior Solution Specialist for
Virtual Cloud Network at VMware.

Credentials: CCIE Service Provider, CCIE Routing & Switching, Nokia NRS II, VCIX-NV.

The posts here are field notes on VCF, end to end:

- Networking: NSX T0/T1 design, BGP traffic engineering, EVPN/VXLAN, NSX
  Federation, DFW, multi-tenancy with NSX Projects, TEP-less VPCs.
- Kubernetes: VKS, the Supervisor, self-service through VCF Automation,
  GitOps with Argo CD.
- Operations: VCF Operations, the offline depot, lifecycle, and the AI
  Assistant running on a private LLM.
- Holodeck: the nested VCF 9.1 lab all of the above is built on. It puts the whole
  stack on one host, which makes it easy to tear down and rebuild as often as an idea needs.

Everything is written up as working configs with the traps called out, because that's
what Sorin wished existed when searching for answers. If a post says a config works, it ran
on the Holodeck first.

Based in Quebec. Posts are in English and French, and Sorin answers questions in either language.
