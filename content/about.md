I'm **Sorin Platon**, a VCF Solution Architect focused on cloud networking across
VMware Cloud Foundation, NSX and Kubernetes. Before Broadcom's acquisition I was a
Senior Solution Specialist for Virtual Cloud Network at VMware.

Credentials: CCIE Service Provider, CCIE Routing & Switching, Nokia NRS II, VCIX-NV.

What you'll find here are field notes: NSX T0/T1 design, BGP traffic engineering,
EVPN/VXLAN, NSX Federation, DFW, multi-tenancy with NSX Projects — written as working
configs with the traps called out, because that's what I wish I'd found when I was
searching.

Nearly everything here is reproduced first on a nested VCF 9.1 Holodeck — the
whole stack on one host, torn down and rebuilt as often as the idea needs. If a
post says a config works, it ran there.

Based in Quebec. I write in English; I'll answer in French too.
