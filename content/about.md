I'm **Sorin Platon**, a VCF Solution Architect. My background is cloud networking
across VMware Cloud Foundation, NSX and Kubernetes; the work now spans the whole
VCF stack. Before Broadcom's acquisition I was a Senior Solution Specialist for
Virtual Cloud Network at VMware.

Credentials: CCIE Service Provider, CCIE Routing & Switching, Nokia NRS II, VCIX-NV.

What you'll find here are field notes on VCF, end to end:

- **Networking** — NSX T0/T1 design, BGP traffic engineering, EVPN/VXLAN, NSX
  Federation, DFW, multi-tenancy with NSX Projects, TEP-less VPCs.
- **Kubernetes** — VKS, the Supervisor, self-service through VCF Automation,
  GitOps with Argo CD.
- **Operations** — VCF Operations, the offline depot, lifecycle, and the AI
  Assistant running on a private LLM.
- **Holodeck** — the nested VCF 9.1 lab everything above is built on: the whole
  stack on one host, torn down and rebuilt as often as the idea needs.

All of it is written as working configs with the traps called out, because that's
what I wish I'd found when I was searching. If a post says a config works, it ran
on the Holodeck first.

Based in Quebec. I write in English; I'll answer in French too.
